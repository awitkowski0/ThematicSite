# FAQ

### Does Thematic work on Forge or NeoForge?

Not yet. Thematic runs on Fabric for Minecraft 1.20.1, and a Forge 1.20.1 port is in progress, along with alpha builds for Minecraft 1.21 on NeoForge and Fabric.

### How many suits are there?

{{suitCount}} across {{collectionCount}} collections. You can [browse all of them](/suits).

### Will you add [character]?

They may already be in the works. Open the mod's settings through Mod Menu and turn off **Hide Suits** to see the suits in progress.

### How do I craft a suit?

Build a Suit Bench, then craft the suit there from its parts. Every [suit's page](/suits) lists what it needs, and the [wiki walkthrough](/wiki) covers the steps in order.

### My suit looks wrong or isn't working

Ask in the Discord. The community and the developers are there to help.

### Is there a server?

Yes. Click **Official Server** in the multiplayer menu, or add `thematic.bond` yourself. See [How to join](/play).

### Something on this site looks wrong

This site is built from the mod's own data, so if a suit's abilities or recipe look wrong here, they are probably wrong in game too. Tell us on Discord and we'll fix it in the mod.

Thematic is a superhero mod. There are {{suitCount}} suits in it right now, from DC, Marvel, *The Boys* and *Invincible*, and you craft all of them in survival.

Superman flies and has heat vision. The Flash is faster than anything else in the game. Green Lantern builds things out of light, and Batman gets by on gadgets and a grapple. We try to make each character play like themselves instead of giving everyone the same three powers.

You can see every suit, its powers and its recipe on the [website](/suits).

![Superman using heat vision](/listing/heat_vision.jpg)

## Features

- {{suitCount}} suits across {{collectionCount}} collections, with comic, movie, show and game versions of most characters
- A starter suit you pick on your first join, which you upgrade as you play
- The Suit Bench, where you craft everything else
- Rarities, shinies, and team bonuses when your friends wear suits from the same collection
- Gadgets and weapons for the heroes who don't have powers
- New ores, mobs, bosses and structures, plus the Speed Force

## How to start

Thematic is a Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.20.1. The only thing it needs is [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api). {{installHint}}

When you join a world you'll get to pick a starter suit. After that, your goal is a Suit Bench:

![The Suit Bench recipe](/listing/suit_bench_recipe.jpg)

At the bench you can look through every suit, check its stats and powers, and craft the one you want.

![Choosing Superman at the Suit Bench](/listing/suit_bench.jpg)

Each power has its own key. You can change them under Controls. The in-game wiki explains the rest, and the same pages are on the [website](/wiki).

![Superman suited up](/listing/superman.jpg)

## FAQ

**Forge? NeoForge?**
Not yet, but we're working on it. A Forge 1.20.1 port is in progress, and there are alpha builds for 1.21 on NeoForge and Fabric.

**Can you add (character)?**
They might already be done. Turn off **Hide Suits** in the mod settings to see the suits we're still working on.

**Something's broken.**
Let us know in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/gXk4AJz8qM).

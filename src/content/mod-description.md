# Thematic

Thematic is a Fabric mod for Minecraft 1.20.1 that adds craftable superhero and supervillain suits — {{suitCount}} of them across {{collectionCount}} collections spanning DC, Marvel, *The Boys*, *Invincible*, and more. Each suit has its own abilities drawn from the character it's based on: flight, super strength, heat vision, super speed, energy constructs, and dozens of others.

## Features

- {{suitCount}} suits across {{collectionCount}} collections (DC, Marvel, *The Boys*, *Invincible*, and original characters)
- Character-specific abilities — Batman's gadgets, Superman's flight and heat vision, and Green Lantern's constructs are all built as their own systems, not variations on one shared power set
- A dedicated crafting station, the Suit Bench, for assembling suits from their component parts
- Custom weapons and gadgets for tech-based characters
- Cosmetic capes and gliders on the suits that use them
- The full, current suit list, with abilities and recipes, is at [thematic.bond/suits](/suits) — generated directly from the mod's data on every update, so it doesn't go stale like a hand-written list does

## Installation

- Requires [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
- Built for Minecraft 1.20.1
- Recommended: [Point Blank](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/point-blank) by vics4games, for gun-based characters
- Fabric only — there's no Forge or NeoForge port, and none planned

## Crafting

Suits are crafted at a Suit Bench, from component parts rather than in one step. Each suit's page at [thematic.bond/suits](/suits) lists its exact recipe; the in-game Patchouli guidebook covers the same information.
